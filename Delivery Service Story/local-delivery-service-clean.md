## A Local Delivery Service That Works Outside the System

## Purpose of the Narrative

Monkey represents a worker who has learned the visible motions of a job without understanding the system that gives those motions meaning. He can collect and deliver packages successfully while remaining disconnected from dispatch, inventory, authorization, billing, custody, and recordkeeping.

The story is designed to model a service that **appears to work locally while gradually causing the physical system and the official system to separate**.

---

## 1. Monkey Observes the Delivery Operation

- Maintenance workers drive a fixed circuit between the **Warehouse**, **Willow Cottage**, the **Bakery**, and **Town Hall**.
- Two delivery trucks are usually parked outside because one of the regular workers is off on any given day.
- Monkey watches the visible routine without understanding dispatch, inventory, authorization, billing, or recordkeeping.

---

## 2. Monkey Takes the Apparently Unused Truck

- A maintenance worker takes one truck, leaving the other behind.
- Monkey takes the remaining truck and follows the route he has observed.
- The next day, the returning maintenance worker uses the truck Monkey drove previously, while Monkey takes the other available truck.
- Because the trucks continue rotating, nobody realizes that an unknown additional driver has inserted himself into the roster.

---

## 3. Monkey Imitates Package Collection

- At each stop, Monkey sees packages in the outgoing area.
- He loads every package onto the truck.
- He does not initially attempt to deliver anything locally.
- At the end of the circuit, he unloads everything at the **Warehouse Receiving Dock**.

---

## 4. Monkey Becomes an Unofficial Collection Service

- Outgoing packages disappear from the stops and arrive at the warehouse.
- From each location’s narrow perspective, the service appears to work.
- Monkey has not been hired, assigned, authorized, entered into the roster, or connected to the official delivery records.
- He is operationally present but administratively nonexistent.

---

## 5. Monkey Recognizes the Willow Cottage Symbol

- While loading the truck at Willow Cottage, Monkey notices that one package bears the same symbol used for Willow Cottage.
- He accidentally connects the symbol on the package with the location where he is standing.
- Instead of carrying that package back to the warehouse, he unloads it at Willow Cottage.
- This becomes his first successful local delivery.

---

## 6. The Successful Delivery Is Rewarded

- Someone at Willow Cottage gives Monkey a banana after he leaves the correctly marked package there.
- Monkey learns a simple association:

> **Willow Cottage symbol + Willow Cottage = unload package + receive banana**

- He does not yet understand destinations as a general system.
- He has learned one rewarded exception to his normal collection routine.

---

## 7. Monkey Repeats the Willow Cottage Behavior

- Monkey continues collecting everything from every outgoing area.
- Packages bearing the Willow Cottage symbol remain on the truck until he reaches Willow Cottage.
- He unloads them there and receives bananas.
- Anything without a symbol he recognizes still goes to the Warehouse Receiving Dock.

---

## 8. Monkey Gradually Discovers the Other Symbols

- Through coincidence, repetition, and successful outcomes, Monkey connects additional package symbols with the Bakery and Town Hall.
- He begins delivering marked packages to those locations.
- His understanding remains a collection of local associations rather than a model of the delivery system.
- Unknown, unmarked, or confusing objects continue to be unloaded at the warehouse.

---

## 9. The Townspeople Discover the Free Service

- People notice that anything placed in an outgoing area will be removed without a request, fee, explanation, or apparent limit.
- They begin using Monkey more frequently.
- Legitimate packages are joined by unwanted belongings, surplus objects, broken materials, and miscellaneous junk.
- The users adapt to the service Monkey has accidentally created.

---

## 10. Monkey Transports the Junk Exactly as Trained

- Junk does not carry a destination symbol Monkey recognizes.
- He therefore treats it like every other unknown object.
- He collects it, completes his circuit, and unloads it at the Warehouse Receiving Dock.
- Monkey is not malfunctioning; he is applying his learned rule correctly to bad or undefined inputs.

---

## 11. The Warehouse Absorbs the Hidden Cost

- The townspeople experience a convenient free removal service.
- Monkey experiences successful completion of his learned routine.
- The warehouse receives increasing quantities of unexplained material.
- Nothing identifies who sent it, why it was moved, whether it is cargo or waste, or who is responsible for it.

---

## 12. The Operational and Official Systems Diverge

- Monkey now performs useful collections and some correct deliveries.
- None of his movements are connected to dispatch, inventory records, billing, custody, or searchable history.
- The physical location of objects increasingly differs from the location represented by the official system.
- The more successfully Monkey operates, the larger the discrepancy becomes.

> **The service works in the world while failing to exist in the system’s memory.**

---

## 13. The Warehouse Robot Detects the Fault

- The robot working inside the warehouse encounters the accumulating unexplained material.
- It detects a growing mismatch between expected warehouse conditions and actual warehouse conditions.
- The warehouse is receiving outputs from a process that does not exist in the official model.
- At this point, the robot begins to analyze the fault.

## Central Question

> **What happens when a process receives inputs from a system and provides useful outputs to that system, but is not connected to the system’s authorization, memory, or controls?**
